Reading the bars. Every colored bar on this page (flow bars, timelines) uses the same three colors — active work, waiting in a queue, and blocked — segment width shows how long each stage actually took, not just that it happened. Flow efficiency = active time ÷ (active + waiting + blocked) — a ticket where work is happening most of the time scores high; one that mostly sits in queues scores low, even if it eventually finishes on schedule.
